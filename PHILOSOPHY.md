## 1. The Core Problem: The Friction of Academic Time

Every semester, 10,000+ university students navigate a fragmented maze of static PDF timetables, mismatched section schedules, disjointed elective allocations, and chaotic group chats asking one simple, repetitive question:

> *"When are you free?"*

Traditional campus schedule systems are built for university administration, not for student life. They treat timetables as compliance documents rather than **fluid, social spacetime matrices**.

The result?
- **Missed Project Collaborations**: High-performing teams waste days trying to coordinate hackathons, assignments, and study sessions.
- **Wasted Overlaps**: Hidden windows where both students have free periods, MOOC slots, or skippable electives go completely unnoticed.
- **Administrative Blind Spots**: Office hours and mentor slots are either overlooked or misunderstood.

WORMHOLE approaches scheduling not as a static ledger, but as a **quantum spacetime network**.

### The 4 Tenets of Wormhole:

1. **Zero Friction, Zero Authentication Barrier**
   - Speed is everything. Students should not need to log into bloated institutional portals to find when their friend in Section 5L is free. Instant client-side execution in `< 50ms`.

2. **Every Second is Categorized by Intent**
   - Not all hours are created equal. Wormhole intelligently differentiates between:
     - **Quantum Free (Tier 3)**: MOOC, Sports, Library, Mentor, and Office Hours.
     - **Flexible Shift (Tier 2)**: Professional Elective I (PE-1).
     - **Low Friction (Tier 1)**: Cognitive & Technical Skills (CTS).
     - **Event Horizon (Tier 0)**: Non-negotiable core classes & laboratory sessions.

3. **Spatial Aesthetics over Bureaucratic Design**
   - No beige spreadsheets. Wormhole delivers a brutalist, cosmic dark-mode interface with interactive particle fields, tactile sound synthesis, and glowing anomalies.

4. **Deterministic Privacy**
   - All computation happens strictly client-side in the browser. Zero telemetry tracking, zero server latency, zero downtime.
