# 🌌 WORMHOLE — Product Philosophy & YC Thesis

> *"Time on campus is not continuous; it is a fragmented quantum lattice. WORMHOLE collapses the lattice into instant alignment."*

---

## 1. The Core Thesis

Every university student suffers from **Spacetime Fragmentation**:
- There are **12 independent sections** in CSE Sem 5 (`5A` through `5L`), running on 12 staggered timetables.
- Cross-section collaboration (hackathon teams, project squads, lunch meetups, study sprints) requires **hundreds of coordinating messages** and sending PDF screenshots.
- Standard university timetables are rigid, confusing, and fail to distinguish between **100% Free periods (Office hours, MOOCs, Sports, Library)** and mandatory academic locks.

**WORMHOLE replaces the PDF timetable with a high-velocity Spacetime Coordination Operating System.**

---

## 2. Student Psychology & The Friction Continuum

Students make decisions based on **Time Friction**:

| Friction Tier | Period Types | Student Behavior & Utility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🟢 **Tier 3: Zero Friction (100% Free)** | **Office Hours**, MOOC, Sports, Library, Mentor | Zero attendance liability. Prime slots for sprints, meetings, canteens, and hackathons. |
| 🟡 **Tier 2: Flexible Shift** | **PE-1 Electives** (ML, Cloud, AI, Security, etc.) | Synchronized across all 12 batches on Tue 7 & Thu 5–6. Sections share classrooms. |
| 🟠 **Tier 1: Low Friction** | **CTS** (Soft Skills / Aptitude) | Proxy-friendly, minimal exam consequence. Good for secondary sprint alignments. |
| 🔴 **Tier 0: Hard Lock** | Core Subjects, Mathematics, Practical Labs | Strict attendance tracking. Impossible to skip without penalty. |

---

## 3. Product Moats & Virality Engine

1. **The Squad Sync Loop**: When one student wants to organize a project meeting, they select `5H + 5L + 5A + 5B` and send a magic link (`wormhole.app/#squad=H,L,A,B`). Every recipient instantly opens the app to see their shared free hours.
2. **The "Free Right Now" Live Radar**: Instant gratification. Open the app at any second during the day and see a live beacon of which sections are chilling right now.
3. **Zero Setup / Zero Sign-in**: Instant load in under 50 milliseconds. No password, no database lag, 100% client-side precision.
4. **Haptic Tactility**: Web Audio micro-feedback and 60 FPS gravitational lensing canvas that feels like an Apple/Linear product.
