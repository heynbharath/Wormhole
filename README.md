## ⚡ Why WORMHOLE?

Traditional university schedules are rigid, static, and disjointed. Students waste hours every week asking *"When are you free?"* trying to align hackathon teams, study groups, and project sprints across 12 different sections.

**WORMHOLE** is an ultra-fast, zero-friction spacetime intersection engine that calculates every shared free period, elective flexibility, and guaranteed campus break in $< 50\text{ms}$.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- 🌀 **The Rift (Dual-Section Overlap Matrix)**: Select any two sections (e.g. 5H × 5L) to instantly extract every ranked overlap window.
- ⏰ **Live "Now" Radar**: Real-time clock tracking pulses the currently active period and shows the exact time remaining until the next class.
- 🟢 **100% Free Office Hours**: Office periods are natively classified as prime free time alongside MOOCs, Sports, and Library sessions.
- 🪐 **Interactive Gravitational Vortex**: 60 FPS hardware-accelerated Canvas wormhole reacting to cursor physics and schedule alignment.
- 🔊 **Native Web Audio Synthesis**: Zero-asset, futuristic tactile audio feedback with an instant mute toggle.
- 📅 **One-Click .ICS Calendar Export**: Export your personalized weekly timetable directly into Google Calendar & Apple Calendar.
- 💎 **Cosmic Glassmorphism UI**: High-contrast, deep-space dark theme engineered for instant readability in bright classrooms or late-night study sessions.
